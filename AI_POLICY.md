# AI Policy for the CHAOSS AI Alignment Working Group

This policy governs the use of AI tools in contributions to the CHAOSS AI Alignment Working Group. It is adapted from the [Fedora AI-Assisted Contributions Policy](https://docs.fedoraproject.org/en-US/council/policy/ai-contribution-policy/), which provides a neutral framing focused on accountability, transparency, and recognized boundaries.

We use the term "AI" loosely to include LLMs, ML, and related technologies.

## 1. Project Contributions with Help from AI

- **Embrace your human side.** Contributing to this working group is hard, especially when overcoming language and cultural barriers. It might be tempting to use AI tools to polish language and "look more professional." We generally prefer people to use their own voice, however imperfect, than to communicate via a bland averaged LLM output.

- **Do NOT post AI/LLM slop in working group communication channels.** Do not post unverified dumps of generated content as a reply to an issue, a draft for a metric definition, a discussion item, or any other working group communication channel. Adding a prefix like "I just wanted to share the AI output with you" or "I asked AI and this is what it said, give or take" to slop does not make it less slop. Do not post it.

- **Do NOT use LLM tools to expand your talking points.** If you want to write a one-line question, write a one-line question, not a generated one-pager. You will be perceived as more professional and trustworthy in open source work if you do not water down your main message.

- **Do NOT use AI/ML tools to score or evaluate submissions** for CfP, funding requests, internships, or related items. It is hard to track and prevent the natural bias included in most AI/ML tools by design, so we will not rely on such tools for evaluation and selection.

- **Disclose AI use in contributions.** If you used AI tools to assist with a contribution (e.g., drafting, editing, organizing), disclose this in your pull request or issue comment. This is a working group about AI alignment — we model the transparency we expect from others.

## 2. AI Tools for Working Group Members

- **Do explore AI/ML/LLM use for accessibility improvements.** Consider use cases like translation, transcribing, and voice generation to make working group participation more accessible.

- **Do NOT enable any AI/ML/LLM assistants by default in working group tooling.** Any such service, especially one sending user data to a remote location, requires explicit informed consent.

## 3. Working Group Data and AI Development

- **Aggressive scraping is strictly prohibited.** Scraping data in a way that causes significant load or costs is prohibited, no matter the cause. If you need to fetch data for a valid reason, please reach out to the working group chairs to figure out an optimal and non-destructive approach.

- **Respect the open nature of working group content.** Much of this working group's non-code content is covered by open licenses. When using open data from the project for research or model training, respect the open nature of that data, share the outcome, and give credit.

## 4. AI in Metrics Development

- **Do NOT use AI tools to generate metric definitions without human review.** Metrics produced by this working group must reflect community values and human judgment. AI may assist with drafting or organizing, but the substantive content must be developed and reviewed by human contributors.

- **Be transparent about AI-assisted analysis.** If AI tools are used to analyze data, generate visualizations, or assist with research in the development of metrics, disclose this in the relevant metric documentation.

## Scope

This policy applies to all contributions to this repository, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other working group communication channels.

## Enforcement

Violations of this policy will be addressed by the working group co-chairs. Repeated or egregious violations may result in removal from the working group. Concerns can be raised by contacting any of the co-chairs listed in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
